  • Agreement allows UC to sell energy on regional grid (WVXU)

    The University of Cincinnati received approval from the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio to sell excess electricity from its 47-megawatt uptown utility plants onto the regional power grid. Under the agreement, Duke Energy will measure the power UC supplies and report it to regional administrator PJM for compensation.

  • Proposed zoning changes could make going solar easier in Cincinnati (WVXU)

    The Cincinnati Planning Commission approved updated zoning rules that explicitly permit residential and commercial solar panels and canopies across most property locations. City Council is expected to vote on the changes in December as part of the Green Cincinnati Plan.

  • Bradner defeats Abrams for Ohio State House District 29 seat (WVXU)

    Democratic challenger Jonathan Bradner defeated incumbent Republican Cindy Abrams in Ohio State House District 29 after the Hamilton County Board of Elections voted to count over 700 provisional ballots. The certified final margin did not require an automatic recount.
